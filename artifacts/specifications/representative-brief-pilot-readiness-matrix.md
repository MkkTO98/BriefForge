## Current verdict

`NOT READY FOR REPRESENTATIVE PILOT`.

## Exact prerequisite: TASK-060

`Representative Static-HTML Projection and Semantic-Conformance Capability` must support only:

1. bounded multiple sections and statements;
2. exact section hierarchy and encounter order;
3. structured values with units/types/source links;
4. one fixed static table expression;
5. qualification/uncertainty target attachment;
6. complete provenance and obligation semantics;
7. deterministic standalone HTML;
8. exact conformance over every admitted field.

Machine-checkable success requires:

- every projected identity appears exactly once at its planned location;
- section parent/order/membership equal the verified projection;
- statement text/posture/order/authority/reference closure equal the verified projection;
- every numeric lexical value, unit, semantic type and value/source reference equal the verified projection;
- every qualification target relation is present and unambiguous;
- every provenance and obligation field is represented and linked;
- no unplanned semantic content or fallback exists;
- two invocations remain byte-identical;
- TASK-052–056 authority boundaries, exactly 11 BCRP kinds and import isolation remain intact.

Human inspection may observe the artifact but creates no visual-quality, usability, accessibility, comprehension or reader-approval evidence.
