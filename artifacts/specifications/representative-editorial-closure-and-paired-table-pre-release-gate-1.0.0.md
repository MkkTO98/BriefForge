# Representative Editorial Closure and Paired-Table Pre-Release Gate

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-08-01
Status: accepted by TASK-064
Authority boundary: authoring contract only; no Candidate or downstream authority

## Purpose

Prevent promotion of a representative Candidate unless every admitted mandatory editorial element has an exact destination or justified exclusion and the quantitative presentation preserves relationship-owned paired rows.

## Inputs

The gate consumes only:

1. an immutable admitted source inventory;
2. a complete editorial-closure ledger;
3. one exact paired-table contract;
4. a non-instantiated source-to-Candidate authoring blueprint;
5. a fresh Candidate proposed by a later authorized task; and
6. independent editorial and mapping review evidence.

TASK-064 supplies items 1–4 and the gate definition. It does not supply item 5.

## Editorial closure requirements

Every admitted source-authored editorial item must have:

- a stable item identity;
- authoritative source artifact and exact locator;
- source-authored meaning;
- `mandatory`, `conditionally-included`, or `excluded` status;
- evidence-backed status reason;
- exact Candidate destination;
- exact rendered destination or explicit exclusion placement;
- relationship and qualification links where applicable;
- exact provenance and obligation attachments;
- analytical-versus-structural classification; and
- closed status.

Mandatory items may not be compressed away. Conditional items must be absent from unconditional topology and may acquire an edge only under their declared condition. Excluded items must remain visible as source-boundary exclusions when omission could mislead.

## Paired-table requirements

The representative quantitative table is exactly:

| Column | Heading |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Relationship case |
| 2 | Raw Pearson coefficient |
| 3 | Raw state |
| 4 | First-difference Pearson coefficient |
| 5 | First-difference state |

It contains exactly seven relationship-owned rows and fourteen paired coefficients. Every row must bind:

- one relationship identity and label;
- one raw value;
- one first-difference value;
- transformation states;
- periods and aligned counts;
- method and package identities;
- source inclusion role and display evidence role;
- classification;
- qualifications; and
- exact source provenance.

Fourteen generic value rows, missing pair members, duplicate relationships, unconsumed values, reordered relationships, or a second table fail closed.

## Identity and attachment requirements

- Every Candidate destination must satisfy BCRP identity grammar and resolve in the blueprint.
- One row-identity namespace is permitted.
- Each row must have an explicit provenance object targeting the row statement and both values.
- Every ledger-declared provenance or obligation attachment must be reciprocally listed against the exact destination in the blueprint.
- Parent/root attachment is not a transitive substitute for an exact target.

## Promotion sequence

A later authorized fresh Candidate must pass, in order:

1. immutable source and inventory revalidation;
2. exact ledger consumption;
3. exact paired-table realization;
4. qualification/provenance/obligation closure;
5. independent editorial and source-to-content mapping review; and
6. promotion authorization.

Any failure stops before Judgment, Ready, Release, projection, renderer invocation, semantic-conformance evidence, Manifest, delivery, or deployment.

## Historical comparison

## Non-authority
