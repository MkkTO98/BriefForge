## Decision standard

Route A is acceptable only if a complete, machine-checkable mapping preserves every governed identity, target, attachment and rendered destination inside all existing TASK-062 profile, strict-schema, projection and renderer constraints. Lower counts alone are not evidence of equivalence.

Route B is acceptable only if it is additive, versioned, bounded, evidence-derived, backward-compatible and no broader than the semantic failures require.

## Route A — editorial-contract revision

### Complete-mapping test

A candidate mapping must simultaneously preserve:

- 109 governed treatments and 74–76 exact statement identities;
- exact six-section topology, including 27–29 interpretation and 32 limitations children;
- 27 ordered qualification edges from ten sources to statements, values and table;
- five distinct obligation meanings, including one 82-target non-transitive attachment;
- eight provenance objects, including one 92-target source attachment and seven row/package attachments;
- lexical coefficient, transformation, period, aligned count, method and package metadata;
- seven relationship-owned paired rows and five exact columns;
- row-owned classifications, roles, qualifications and provenance;
- exact hierarchy and encounter order.

### Falsification result

No complete mapping exists. The following are independently decisive and cannot be repaired by prose compression:

1. At least 74 exact statement identities exceed 32.
2. Required 27/29 and 32-child leaves exceed 17 children and 16 leaf statements.
3. Provenance and obligation authored children are invalid child types.
4. Twenty-seven qualification edges exceed 16, reuse sources and target values/table that TASK-062 forbids.
5. One obligation needs 82 exact targets versus 16.
6. Provenance has no target topology or commit/snapshot/package fields.
7. Required value metadata and exact lexical forms have no governed fields; leading-zero lexical payloads are rejected.
8. Table/case schemas have no governed columns, relationship-row identity or state cells.
9. The fixed renderer always emits fourteen slot-owned rows and the wrong headings.
10. Supporting semantics render in generic closure rather than their governed row destinations.

Merging statements would erase stable identities and attachment endpoints. Packing metadata into prose would make it untyped and unenforceable. Splitting qualifications or obligations would change governed identities. Renderer output cannot be changed by Candidate wording. Therefore Route A is conclusively rejected—not merely unproven.

## Route B — versioned terminal capability

Route B is necessary because admission schemas, projection DTOs and renderer behavior all require new semantics. A profile-only limit increase or renderer-only patch is insufficient.

- one new exact terminal profile/version;
- one versioned strict semantic/projection schema;
- bounded raised cardinalities;
- one-to-many qualification topology over statements, values and table;
- typed obligation, provenance and value metadata;
- relationship-owned paired-row table representation;
- one fixed deterministic renderer for that representation;
- independent parsed conformance;
- one additive exact dispatch tuple through Manifest admission;
- unchanged lifecycle, operational-authority separation, delivery, deployment and TASK-056 boundaries.
